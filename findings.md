# Retail Sales Analysis — Key Findings

## 1. Regional Performance
- Highest-revenue region: North
- Lowest-revenue region: West
- Region with the highest average order value: North

## 2. Product Performance
- Best-selling product by units: Notebook Set
- Highest-revenue product: Smartphone
- Product with the lowest sales: Wireless Mouse

## 3. Category Performance
- Highest-revenue category: Electronics
- Category with the most units sold: Electronics

## 4. Customer Behaviour
- Highest-spending customer: Musa Adeyemi,
- with approximately 2.1 million in revenue.
- Customer with the most orders: Musa Adeyemi
- Number of VIP customers: 272

## 5. Advanced Analysis
- Number of customers meeting the high-value criteria: 79
- Notable observations:
- 1.High-value customers were concentrated almost equally
- between the Central and North regions.
- 2.No customers from the South met the high-value criteria,
- suggesting a potential difference in customer purchasing behaviour or regional sales performance.
- 3.Musa Adeyemi was both the highest-spending customer and the customer
- with the most orders, indicating strong purchasing activity.

## 6. Conclusion: 
The analysis identified North as the highest-revenue region
and Electronics as the strongest-performing product category.
Smartphones generated the highest product revenue,
while Notebook Sets recorded the highest sales volume.
Customer analysis identified Musa Adeyemi as the highest-spending customer and the customer with the most orders. Furthermore, 79 customers met the advanced high-value criteria, with nearly equal representation from Central and North and no qualifying customers from South.
